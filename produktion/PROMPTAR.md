# FreshFix reklamfilm – produktionspaket för AI-video

Varje klipp har en **startbild** i `startbilder/` (exakt dina karaktärer, rätt bildutsnitt).
Ladda upp startbilden i ett AI-verktyg med läget **bild till video** (*image to video*), klistra in prompten och generera.
Promptarna är på engelska eftersom verktygen förstår engelska bäst. Den svenska repliken står med så att munrörelserna får rätt längd.

**Gratisalternativ:** Kling AI, Hailuo (MiniMax) och Vidu ger gratis krediter varje dag. Pixverse och Pika har gratisnivåer.
Välj 5 sekunder och bildformatet 4:3 (eller 1:1) om det finns. Klippen kortas till rätt längd vid monteringen.

Lägg de färdiga klippen i `produktion/klipp/` med samma namn som startbilden, till exempel `01_kast.mp4`.

**Gemensam stil (lägg till sist i varje prompt):**
`stylized 3D animated film, PS2-era game cutscene look, bright desert sunlight, deep blue sky, cinematic, natural human body motion, consistent character, no text, no watermark`

**Negativ prompt (om verktyget har fält för det):**
`text, subtitles, watermark, logo, extra fingers, deformed hands, face morphing, different character, blurry`

## Promptar

**01 – Kastet**
The blond young man in the dark suit stands in the desert holding an open cardboard box in front of him while talking confidently, then casually tosses the box to the left out of frame with one hand. The short boy in the striped sweater and the green-headed man in the background watch and shift their weight. Static wide shot.

**02 – Han fångar lådan**
The young man with the Santa hat and the metal fridge-like vest catches a cardboard box rising into his hands at chest height, glances down at it, then looks up suspiciously at the camera and says angrily "Vem fan är du?". Slow camera push-in.

**03, 08, 14 – Underifrån (hjältebild)**
Low-angle hero shot from below of the blond man in the suit against a deep blue sky with sun glare. He is silent for a beat, then turns his head slightly toward camera and speaks slowly and menacingly with a confident smirk, natural lip movement and blinking. Subtle slow camera drift.

**04 – Närbild**
Close-up of the blond man in the suit, head slightly tilted, speaking intensely and proudly, small head movements, natural blinking and mouth movement, slow push-in.

**05 – Arg**
Extreme close-up of the young man with the Santa hat squinting angrily and snapping "Varför ljuger du?", head jerks slightly forward.

**06 – Över axeln (10 s, dela gärna upp i två klipp à 5 s)**
Over-the-shoulder shot: the blurred back of a Santa-hat head in the left foreground, the blond man in the suit on the right talking to him. He explains calmly with hand gestures, raises one hand palm up to his chest saying "I", then gestures toward the boys behind him, leans forward and looks down, then looks back up and points at the box in the other man's hands. The striped-sweater boy and the green-headed man stand in the background. Handheld camera feel.

**07 – Profil**
Wide side view in the desert: the Santa-hat man on the left holds a cardboard box at chest height and listens. The man in the suit on the right faces him and gestures with his forearm while explaining, counting on two fingers. Static camera.

**09 – Produkten**
An open cardboard FreshFix box with instruction sheet, a metal grille container and a filter pouch lies on the desert sand. Slow cinematic push-in, light breeze, sun glare. Keep the product exactly as in the image.

**10 – Chock**
Close-up of the Santa-hat man frozen in shock, mouth slightly open, slow blink, tiny head pull-back.

**11 – Över axeln 2**
Over-the-shoulder shot: blurred Santa-hat head in the foreground left. The blond man in the suit first looks away to the right, then turns back toward him, says something with a knowing look and crosses his arms confidently.

**12 – Ögonen**
Extreme close-up of the blond man's eyes, very slow push-in, intense stare, one slow blink, tiny eyebrow raise.

**13 – Du är FreshFix**
Medium close-up of the Santa-hat man holding the box at chest height, the suit man's back blurred in the right foreground. He hesitates, swallows, then says reluctantly "Du är FreshFix".

**15 – Final**
Wide desert shot. The man in the suit takes one confident step toward the camera, then points down toward the bottom of the frame with his index finger while talking. The Santa-hat man holds the box on the left, the striped-sweater boy and the green-headed man stand on the right, shifting their weight naturally.

---

**Tips:**
- Generera 2–3 versioner av varje klipp och välj den bästa.
- Byter AI:n ansikte på någon, kör om eller lägg till "keep the exact same face as in the image".
- Klipp 9 (produkten) och 12 (ögonen) fungerar också bra som de är i den nuvarande filmen.
